## How to Use This Module Under Time Pressure

1. **Know your target role, short on time**: open that role's interview map, work top-down through Must Know, using [[ML Cheatsheet]] as a fast formula lookup for anything half-remembered.
2. **General cram, more time available**: read [[ML Cheatsheet]] end to end (§1–24), then [[Common Interview Questions - ML]] and [[Common Mistakes in ML]] for the framing/pitfall layer on top of the formulas.
3. **Stuck on one specific concept**: every wikilink above resolves to the full atomic concept note with worked examples and its own interview Q&A — the cheatsheet and role maps are indexes into that content, not replacements for it.

## One-line Summary

> This module is the entry point for interview prep, not a parallel knowledge base — the cheatsheet and decision guides compress the whole subject into fast-reference form, and the five role maps let you approach the same canonical concept notes from whichever direction your actual interview does.
