# Research — Overview

## Scope

Everything related to reading, doing, and writing up research — separate from applied work in `02_Projects`. This is where paper notes (using `99_Templates/Paper_Template.md`), research methodology, and your own research ideas/threads live.

## Planned sub-areas (fill in as your research phase starts)

1. Paper Notes — one note per paper read, using the existing Paper Template, cross-linked to the atomic concept notes the paper depends on (e.g. a paper using permutation tests links to [[Permutation Test]])
2. Research Methodology — literature review technique, how to read a paper efficiently, reproducibility practices
3. Open Questions / Ideas — your own research threads, hypotheses, half-formed ideas worth revisiting
4. Writing — abstract/paper writing conventions, common reviewer feedback patterns

## Why this folder exists

You mentioned research is the next phase to add. This scaffolds the home for it now so paper notes and research threads have a stable place from day one, rather than getting stuffed into ML & DL or Projects and needing to be moved later.

## Connections

- Paper notes here will link out into whichever subject's atomic concepts they use (ML & DL, Statistics & Experimental Design, etc.) rather than duplicating those concepts
- Projects (`02_Projects`) — applied work is separate from research reading/writing, but the two will cross-link constantly
