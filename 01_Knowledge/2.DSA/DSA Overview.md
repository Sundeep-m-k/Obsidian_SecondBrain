# DSA — Overview

## Scope

Classic Data Structures & Algorithms — the interview-prep backbone. Kept as its own subject (not folded into CS Foundations) because it has its own learning path and its own huge body of practice problems.

## Planned sub-areas (fill in as studied)

1. Arrays, Strings & Hashing
2. Linked Lists, Stacks & Queues
3. Trees & Binary Search Trees
4. Heaps & Priority Queues
5. Graphs (BFS, DFS, shortest paths, topological sort)
6. Sorting & Searching
7. Recursion & Backtracking
8. Dynamic Programming
9. Greedy Algorithms
10. Meta: Pattern Recognition — which technique for which problem shape (mirrors the "21.Meta & Interview Revision" pattern already used in ML & DL)

## Why this folder exists

Distinct from [[1.CS Foundations/CS Foundations Overview|CS Foundations]]: DSA is about solving bounded problems efficiently; CS Foundations is about how the underlying machine and software systems work.
